# Glossary

**Account**
The identity used to sign in. One person can belong to several workspaces or groups.

**Account Manager**
A workspace role that can manage permitted members and operational records below the Owner level.

**Attachment / Performance Material**
A chart, lyric, tab, sheet-music file, PDF, image, ChordPro text, audio file, MIDI file, or related song resource.

**Canonical store**
The authoritative Studio Leviathan cloud/web data. Android synchronizes an operational local copy.

**Crew**
Studio Leviathan's controlled assistance and workflow system. AI-backed tasks run through the main server, not as unsupervised local Android administration.

**Guest link**
A temporary scoped link for a recipient who may not have an account. Possession of the private URL can grant access until expiration or revocation.

**Kit**
A reusable collection of equipment and quantities.

**Leviathan Live**
The performance environment for set lists, charts, lyrics, timing, playback, metronome, controls, and connected devices.

**Local Live**
A host-authoritative local-network session used when participating devices share Wi-Fi but internet service is unavailable.

**Material access**
Rules determining which shared songs, charts, lyrics, or stems a member can receive. Usually inherited from group/role with explicit exceptions.

**Member overlay**
Personal notes or preferences layered over shared canonical material without changing other members' source.

**Module**
A licensed operational area controlling which record categories and functions a workspace can use.

**Offline packet**
The locally available records and files needed for an event or performance.

**Queued change**
A local edit saved on Android but not yet confirmed by the server.

**Registered share**
A scoped share associated with another Studio Leviathan account, separate from workspace membership.

**Routing profile**
A mapping from virtual audio buses to physical outputs on a particular or compatible audio interface.

**Song section**
A named timed part such as Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge, or Solo used by the marker strip and timed navigation.

**Stem**
One audio component of a multitrack song, such as click, guide, backing vocals, instruments, or effects.

**Virtual bus**
A named logical audio destination that can be routed differently for each hardware profile.

**Wear companion**
The non-standalone Wear OS application that displays and controls selected Leviathan Live state from the paired Android performance device.

**Workspace**
An individual musician, band, studio, tour/production organization, educational group, worship group, or other organization with its own records, members, modules, and permissions.
